🎮 Gaming & Media

I like old mil-sim and RTS games, as well as point-and-click adventure games like Dark Seed. I'm obsessed with the Xbox 360 era of gaming, specifically games like Crysis and Halo. I love a lot of games because of their atmosphere — the S.T.A.L.K.E.R. series is a perfect example.

I'm also a fan of so many different kinds of media that some people might even say I've played every game and watched every movie ever made. You can find a list of things I'm still actively into in the interests section.

💬 Social & Contact

One of the things I really enjoy is talking to people, but I'm too scared to actually talk to anyone and I have basically zero social skills. Feel free to message me on Discord or Twitter/X, or add me on Steam to play games together — my Steam is linked.

✨ Other Interests

A few more things I like: winter, learning about history, I'm fascinated by war and old technology, and I love looking at weird art — anything H.R. Giger made, for example. I'm also a very active music listener with extremely niche taste, so please don't bother recommending anything to me.

Have fun browsing the site!
